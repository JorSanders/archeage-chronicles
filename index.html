My timer for the rifts in ArcheAge Chronicles. Crimson Rift starts at 03:00, 11:00 and 19:00 UTC, Brutal Nightmare at 04:00, 12:00 and 20:00 UTC, and both last 30 minutes. The daily reset is at 04:00 UTC. This page shows them in your own time zone and can send a browser notification 10 minutes before each rift starts.

Notifications come from this page itself, so they only work while it is open in a tab. Nothing leaves your browser.

Provided free of charge, without tracking. For the love of the game.
